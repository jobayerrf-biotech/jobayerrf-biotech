# 🌱 Aloe vera Root-Associated Microbiome

**Project type:** MSc thesis research  
**Approach:** Illumina 16S rRNA (V3–V4) amplicon sequencing  
**Focus:** Bacterial community diversity in healthy and root-rot-affected *Aloe vera*

## Research question

How do the bacterial communities associated with *Aloe vera* rhizosphere soil and root endosphere vary with plant health status?

## Study overview

Samples represent rhizosphere and root-endosphere compartments from healthy and root-rot-affected plants. Four samples were submitted for sequencing, of which three passed quality requirements; this limits the strength and balance of downstream comparisons.

## Analysis workflow

1. Review read quality and process 16S rRNA amplicon sequences using **QIIME2**.
2. Infer amplicon sequence variants (**ASVs**) and assign taxonomy.
3. Summarize taxonomic community composition.
4. Explore alpha diversity with **Chao1, Shannon, and Simpson** indices.
5. Explore community dissimilarity with **Bray–Curtis** measures.
6. Interpret exploratory results in the context of plant compartment and health status.

## Interpretation and limitations

- The small number of usable samples makes statistical comparisons exploratory.
- Disease-associated differences in bacterial profiles do **not** establish microbial causality.
- All figures and result statements should be linked to verified output files before public release.
