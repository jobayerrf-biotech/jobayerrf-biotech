# 🔬 Low-Cost Microfluidic Device Fabrication

**Project type:** BSc research  
**Focus:** Photolithography-based microfluidic device fabrication for bioassay applications

## Objective

Explore a rapid, accessible and cost-effective fabrication approach for small microfluidic features and prototype bioassay systems.

## Research approach

The project explored photolithography-based preparation of microfluidic structures and proof-of-concept fluid-flow testing. It combined experimental design, fabrication, and hands-on validation of prototype features.

## Research relevance

This work strengthened skills in experimental method design and evaluation, which complement later interests in molecular biotechnology and computational microbiology.
