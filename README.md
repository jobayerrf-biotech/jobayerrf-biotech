## About me

I am **Jobayer Hossain Rafi**, a biotechnology researcher with training in **Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Bangladesh**. My research interests center on **microbial ecology, metagenomics, microbial genomics, and host–microbe interactions** across biological and environmental systems.

My experience spans molecular biology and NGS-related laboratory workflows, along with computational microbiome profiling. I am especially interested in connecting **community composition**, **ecological context**, and **functional potential** through careful, reproducible analyses.

## Research interests

- 🌍 **Microbial ecology:** community assembly, diversity, and environmental drivers
- 🧫 **Host–microbe interactions:** healthy and disease-associated microbiomes
- 🧬 **Microbial genomics:** taxonomic and functional characterization using sequencing data
- 💻 **Reproducible bioinformatics:** transparent methods, scripts, metadata, and software versions

## Research principles

I aim to communicate results with **clear assumptions and limitations**. In particular, I distinguish associations from causal explanations and predictions of functional potential from direct experimental evidence.

The individual project pages document the scientific aims and analytical methods. As projects progress, I plan to add shareable, validated workflows and figures with suitable metadata, citations, and reproducibility notes.
