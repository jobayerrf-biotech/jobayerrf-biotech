About Me
I am a biotechnology researcher with a background in Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. My research interests include microbial ecology, metagenomics, microbial genomics, and host–microbe interactions across biological and environmental systems.
I have hands-on experience with molecular biology and NGS-related workflows, alongside bioinformatics analysis of microbial communities.
